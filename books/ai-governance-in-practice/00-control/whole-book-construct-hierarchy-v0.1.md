# Whole-Book Construct Hierarchy v0.1

**Book:** *AI Governance in Practice*  
**Date:** 2026-11-19  
**Status:** **CONTROLLED FOR WHOLE-BOOK AUTHOR REVISION**

## Governing hierarchy

### Central organising idea

**Meaningful Accountability**

The book's central organising idea is that accountability remains meaningful only where organisational design preserves the practical conditions required to understand, challenge, intervene and remain answerable for AI-mediated work.

### Problem diagnosis

**Accountability–Control Gap**

A reader-facing diagnosis of the mismatch between accountability assigned to a person, role or institution and the practical control that can realistically be exercised.

### Primary organisational diagnostic

**Meaningful Accountability Test**

The five-condition diagnostic covering capability, visibility, authority, intervention capacity and accountability alignment.

### Authority-design tool

**Delegated Authority Map**

The practical tool for making Function, Permission, Boundary, Control, Recourse and Accountability visible.

### Enabling-governance lens

**Governance as Permission**

The lens for designing explicit, proportionate and reviewable operating room, with authority, evidence, exception, escalation and withdrawal conditions.

### Evidence and assurance spine

**Evolved Governance Chain**

The non-linear diagnostic traceability spine:

> **Purpose → Authority → Boundary → Execution → Evidence → Evaluation → Learning**

Accountability, risk and exposure, human judgement, controls, monitoring and change remain cross-cutting conditions. Assurance remains the conclusion produced through evaluation, not another link.

### Descriptive synthesis

**authority drift**

Lower-case descriptive language for gradual movement between originally approved authority and authority exercised in practice.

### Supporting syntheses

- **governance infrastructure** — the enactment layer through which governance boundaries become operational;
- **Capability as Governance** — the proposition that organisational capability is one mechanism through which governance operates.

These are supporting arguments rather than peer frameworks or stand-alone models.

### Final integration

**Chapter 10 runtime-action propositions**

The final chapter integrates the approved architecture at the point permission becomes action. It introduces no new named framework.

## Control implications

1. Visual hierarchy must follow this structure.
2. Marketing copy must not present every construct as an equal framework.
3. First-use language must distinguish diagnosis, test, tool, lens, spine, descriptive synthesis and proposition.
4. *Action path* remains lower-case descriptive language.
5. The operating-system metaphor remains the book-level organising metaphor, not an additional construct.
6. No construct may be presented as externally validated, legally determinative or certified unless the manuscript expressly states otherwise and evidence supports that claim.
