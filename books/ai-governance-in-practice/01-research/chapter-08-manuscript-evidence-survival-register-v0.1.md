## Repair E8-01 — Exception-path evidence visibility

### Current manuscript movement

The manuscript states that an exception is a governed response to a condition the ordinary rule cannot resolve and requires reason, authority, scope, duration, evidence and review.

### Evidence position

This is a defensible synthesis of:

- Adler and Borys on repair and flexibility in enabling formalisation;
- NIST on escalation, course correction, conditional decisions and lifecycle action;
- responsible-innovation scholarship on responsiveness when assumptions or conditions change.

### Required repair

Add a proportionate citation at the exception-definition movement, for example:

> An exception is not an escape hatch from governance. It is a governed response to a condition the ordinary rule cannot resolve. It requires a reason, authority, scope, duration, evidence and review (Adler & Borys, 1996; NIST, 2023; Stilgoe, Owen & Macnaghten, 2013).

The citation may be lighter if the surrounding prose already carries one of these sources, but the warrant must remain traceable from the manuscript itself.

## Three-Layer Evidence Test

### Evidence establishes

- formalisation may enable or coerce depending on design and use;
- well-designed controls can support repair, transparency, flexibility and coordination;
- hierarchy and information use can erode enabling qualities;
- risk governance can be differentiated according to context and materiality;
- conditional approval, course correction and withdrawal are legitimate governance mechanisms;
- cumulative administrative burden can weaken outcomes and encourage workarounds;
- high-reliability organising combines disciplined baselines with adaptation and relevant expertise;
- responsible innovation requires responsiveness and has practical limits.

### Reasonable interpretation

- governance should convert risk appetite into usable decision boundaries;
- conditional permission can be stronger than either indefinite delay or unqualified approval;
- authorised exception pathways can strengthen control;
- escalation should connect uncertainty with relevant expertise and decision authority;
- governance burden should be tested against purpose and material risk;
- permission should remain monitored, reviewable and revocable.

### Gardner synthesis

- Governance as Permission;
- well-designed governance creates legitimate room to act;
- the opposite of weak governance is not necessarily more governance;
- permission to act is not permission to ignore risk;
- permission without evidence becomes optimism.

The layers remain reconstructable after E8-01 and E8-02 are closed.
