## About the Author

**Alec Gardner** is the Director of Research and Insight at Nexus BMG, where his work focuses on leadership, organisational capability, workforce transformation and the responsible integration of artificial intelligence into working life.

His professional career spans more than three decades in multinational organisations, including senior leadership responsibilities across the Asia-Pacific region and membership of a global human resources leadership board. This experience has given him a practical understanding of the challenges organisations face when translating strategy, governance and accountability into everyday decisions.

Alec's multidisciplinary background encompasses electrical engineering, adult education, human resource management, business administration and higher education. His work brings together an understanding of organisational systems, human behaviour, leadership and professional capability.

More recently, his research and professional interests have increasingly focused on the relationship between artificial intelligence, human judgement, delegated authority and organisational responsibility.

Through Nexus BMG, Alec explores how organisations can move beyond AI adoption as a technology initiative towards building the leadership capability, operating controls and governance arrangements necessary for responsible and effective implementation.

*AI Governance in Practice* reflects his conviction that meaningful accountability requires more than assigning responsibility. People must also retain the knowledge, authority and practical ability to understand, challenge and influence the decisions for which they are accountable.
