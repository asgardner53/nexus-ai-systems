## Copyedit boundaries preserved

The unified copyedit does not:

- reopen approved chapter architecture;
- change the book's central argument;
- strengthen or universalise an evidence-dependent claim;
- alter approved construct definitions or provenance boundaries;
- introduce a new framework;
- add a separate conclusion;
- remove the composite-case disclosure;
- weaken the final line.
