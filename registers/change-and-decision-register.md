## Decision entry requirements

Each future entry should record:

1. the decision date;
2. the decision category;
3. the approved decision;
4. the reason for the decision;
5. the approving authority;
6. the affected documents, Studios or repositories;
7. implementation status;
8. related commit, pull request or controlled document where applicable.

## Categories

Use one of the following where practical:

- Governance standard
- Studio classification
- Studio approval
- Repository and document control
- Plugin or connector approval
- Doctrine or architecture control
- Privacy or security control
- Incident response
- Material workflow change
- Suspension, retirement or supersession

## Register rules

1. Material decisions made in conversation must be transferred to this register.
2. Draft proposals must not be recorded as approved decisions.
3. A superseded decision must remain visible with its replacement identified.
4. GitHub commit and pull-request history may support the evidence trail but does not replace the governance entry.
5. AIRBOK doctrine and architecture decisions must also be reflected in the AIRBOK repository's controlled registers.
