## 4. Reviewer competence confirmation

Before either reviewer is treated as satisfying IR-2, the review record must confirm that the reviewer has sufficient competence to assess the relevant governance questions.

Competence may be demonstrated through relevant experience in one or more of:

- governance;
- risk;
- assurance;
- AI governance;
- compliance;
- organisational controls;
- professional standards;
- audit/review;
- systems assurance; or
- comparable executive/board oversight.

The final IR-2 record must document the competence basis rather than assume it from title alone.

## 5. Conflict-of-interest confirmation

Before review begins, the reviewer must confirm any material conflict of interest or prior involvement that could weaken independence.

A disclosed conflict does not automatically disqualify the reviewer, but the final review record must state whether independence remains sufficient.
