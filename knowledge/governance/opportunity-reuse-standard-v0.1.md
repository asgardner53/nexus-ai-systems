## Purpose

Define how useful knowledge is converted into downstream opportunities without confusing commercial, editorial or product potential with evidence strength.

## Core rule

**Opportunity value and evidence maturity are separate controls.**

A source or insight may have high commercial, publishing or learning value while still remaining Draft / Candidate or requiring verification.

No opportunity signal may silently upgrade:
- claim status;
- document status;
- knowledge maturity;
- AIRBOK doctrine status;
- regulatory authority;
- client recommendation status.

## Four opportunity signals

### Knowledge signal
The source contributes a reusable concept, model, pattern, risk, decision rule or implementation insight.

### Commercial signal
The source indicates a potential service, diagnostic, advisory offer, workshop, product feature or client need.

### Publishing signal
The source suggests a viable article, newsletter, LinkedIn post, ebook section, case study, visual or speaking topic.

### Doctrine signal
The source raises a concept that may warrant deeper AIRBOK research. A doctrine signal is only a research trigger and does not create doctrine.

## Reuse record

Each material opportunity should record:
- opportunity ID;
- originating knowledge object or source synthesis;
- opportunity signal type;
- proposed destination;
- proposed asset or action;
- evidence maturity of the originating knowledge;
- additional verification required;
- owner;
- priority;
- status;
- human approval requirement.

## Status

Use:
- Identified
- Triaged
- In development
- Ready for approval
- Approved for execution
- Published / delivered
- Deferred
- Closed

Opportunity status does not alter knowledge status.

## Prioritisation

Evaluate opportunities using:
- strategic relevance;
- audience/client value;
- uniqueness;
- evidence readiness;
- effort;
- time sensitivity;
- commercial potential;
- reuse potential across multiple channels.

Avoid false precision. A qualitative High / Medium / Low rating is sufficient unless a specific scoring model is later approved.

## Verification gate

Before an opportunity becomes an external publication, client recommendation, formal training claim, governance rule or AIRBOK research conclusion:
1. check the originating knowledge maturity;
2. verify material factual claims to the required materiality level;
3. refresh time-sensitive claims;
4. check contradictions and boundary conditions;
5. apply the relevant human approval gate.

## AIRBOK boundary

An AIRBOK opportunity signal means only:
**“consider this for AIRBOK research.”**

It must not be interpreted as:
- approved doctrine;
- emerging doctrine;
- controlled terminology;
- chapter inclusion;
- assessment or credential requirement.

Those require the AIRBOK repository and its separate governance process.
