## 7. Contradiction and evidence analysis

### Contradiction A — Stated approval expectation vs informal trials

**Statement 1:** Manager approval is expected before a new AI tool enters a live workflow.

**Statement 2:** Managers sometimes trial tools informally before central review.

**Assessment:** Material contradiction in implementation, not necessarily in policy wording.

**Evidence status:** UNRESOLVED pending documentary and sample-based verification.

**Assurance impact:** The existence of an approval rule cannot be treated as evidence that the control operates consistently.

### Contradiction B — Security review list vs business-use control

**Statement 1:** Technology maintains a list of reviewed tools.

**Statement 2:** Business teams are not required to check the list before using tools.

**Assessment:** The control may exist at a technical review level without an effective consumption or enforcement mechanism.

**Evidence status:** VERIFIED as an interview-level contradiction; operating-effectiveness conclusion remains pending corroboration.

**Assurance impact:** A register by itself may not demonstrate that unapproved tools are prevented or detected.

### Evidence gap C — Guidance issued vs staff understanding

**Statement:** AI guidance has been issued.

**Gap:** No evidence from the interview that staff receipt, understanding or application is systematically verified.

**Evidence status:** PROVISIONAL control gap.

**Assurance impact:** Communication evidence alone is insufficient to conclude effective adoption.
