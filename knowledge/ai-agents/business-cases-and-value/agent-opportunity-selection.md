# Selecting Suitable AI-Agent Opportunities

## Document status
- **Status:** Candidate
- **Owner:** Alec Gardner
- **Knowledge domain:** AI agents / business cases and value

## Knowledge statement
AI-agent initiatives should begin with a clearly defined work problem or workflow gap rather than with a preferred technology.

## Supported interpretation
Tasks and workflows are stronger candidates for agentic support when they combine several of the following:
- repetitive or high-volume work;
- information retrieval or synthesis;
- multiple sequential steps;
- context-dependent decisions within bounded rules;
- coordination across tools or data sources;
- clear measurable outcomes;
- a practical human escalation or approval path.

Some deterministic processes may be better suited to conventional automation where fixed rules, precision and predictability are more important than adaptive reasoning.

## Business-case frame
Assess:
1. current problem or performance gap;
2. expected benefit;
3. implementation and operating cost;
4. material risks;
5. required controls;
6. technology and data dependencies;
7. human resources and ownership;
8. measurable outcome and review period.

## Boundary conditions
Vendor ROI claims, platform capability and pricing are time-sensitive and should be independently verified before use in a formal business case.
