## Citation-analysis record

Scite verified eight academic references used or selected for the audit, including the two decoupling sources, three current AI-governance implementation sources and the 28-organisation public-sector study. No retractions, expressions of concern or predatory-journal flags were detected.

Scite surfaced one later contrasting citation concerning Westphal and Zajac in a different institutional context. The citation does not overturn policy-practice decoupling; it reinforces the need to avoid universalising when and how decoupling occurs.

The source-decision record includes nine selected sources and six exclusions. Excluded items were recent preprints, redundant frameworks or sector-specific studies that did not improve the general organisational evidence position.
