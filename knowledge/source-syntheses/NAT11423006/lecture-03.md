## Reusable concepts identified

1. Start with a genuine business problem rather than selecting an AI tool first.
2. Strong agent opportunities often involve repetitive, information-heavy or multi-step work.
3. Agentic AI adoption is moving toward embedded workflows, multi-agent coordination and broader organisational integration.
4. A sound AI-agent proposal should connect expected benefits with costs, risks, controls, implementation requirements and measurable outcomes.
5. Recommendations need to be communicated in a way that supports stakeholder decisions.

## Five retained takeaways
1. Start with the business problem, not the AI tool.
2. Good agent opportunities involve repeatable, information-heavy or multi-step work.
3. Agentic AI is moving toward embedded workflows and multi-agent systems.
4. AI proposals need measurable value, cost, risk and controls.
5. A recommendation matters only if stakeholders can understand and decide from it.
