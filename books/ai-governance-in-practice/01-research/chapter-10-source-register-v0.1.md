## Currency and verification notes

- NIST, allied cyber guidance, Australian AI Safety Institute, OECD, A2A, MCP, OWASP and UK AISI materials are fast-moving and require publication-stage recheck.
- PDF-derived claims must be verified against page images where layout, figures or tables are material.
- Security-benchmark rates remain confined to tested models, agents, tools, attacks and environments.
- Protocol specifications establish protocol behaviour, not legal or organisational authority.
- EU AI Act claims must use the relevant consolidated date and staged commencement position.
- Final Harvard Australian reference entries must preserve exact titles, issuing bodies, dates, DOI or stable locator, and access date where required.
