## Central argument

AI governance is not primarily a compliance mechanism or a collection of policies. It is the organisational operating system that connects strategy, risk, human judgement, accountability, data, controls, capability and assurance so that AI can be adopted with confidence.

## Primary reader

Executives, directors, managers, HR and workforce leaders, risk and governance professionals, transformation leaders and people responsible for introducing AI into organisations.

## Reader promise

The book should move the reader from asking:

> What AI policy do we need?

to asking:

> What organisational system must exist for people and AI to act responsibly, accountably and effectively?

## Core intellectual chain

**AI use → risk → principle → control → accountable owner → evidence → monitoring → improvement**
