## Purpose

This reconciliation converts the chapter-level evidence records into one whole-book citation and reference control. It tests the controlled manuscript for:

- complete in-text citation families;
- consistent author and institutional names;
- correct publication years;
- one canonical reference entry for each retained citation;
- removal or replacement of citations that could not be verified to publication standard;
- preservation of jurisdictional, methodological and provenance qualifications;
- current verification of fast-moving government, standards and agentic-AI sources;
- compatibility with the unified copyedit.

The reconciliation does not reopen approved Gardner synthesis merely because a source name or year has changed. A return to a substantive evidence gate is required only where the corrected source no longer supports the material claim.

## Sources and verification method

The review used:

1. the controlled source map and every approved front-matter, part and chapter file;
2. the existing chapter research briefs, evidence registers and manuscript-survival registers;
3. primary or official pages for NIST, OECD, Australian Government, the European Union, ISO, IIA, IAASB, PCAOB, COSO, UK Government, UK AI Security Institute, Australian AI Safety Institute, A2A and MCP;
4. publisher, DOI, conference-proceedings and university records for academic sources;
5. current official Microsoft customer stories for the Cemex and Wipro examples;
6. Bloomberg's sourced Samsung report for the 2023 workplace-use example.

Scite MCP could not be rerun during this pass because its connected-call allowance had been exhausted. Earlier Scite-supported chapter work remains in the project record. The final publication-verification gate retains a retraction/editorial-notice scan after access resets or through an equivalent authoritative index. No source with a known retraction, expression of concern or material correction is knowingly retained.

## Citation and reference rules locked for copyedit

1. Use Harvard Australian author–date form.
2. Use `&` inside parenthetical citations and `and` in prose.
3. Use `et al.` for four or more authors in text unless naming the group is necessary for clarity.
4. Use `ISO/IEC`, not `ISO`, for ISO/IEC 42001 and 42006.
5. Use the current organisational name in the reference entry and the manuscript's approved short form in text.
6. Distinguish same-year institutional publications with letters consistently in both citations and references.
7. Do not attach citations ambiguously to Gardner synthesis; make the interpretive transition visible.
8. Retain DOI identifiers where available; use official URLs for institutional and web sources.
9. Do not add access dates to DOI journal articles; use access dates for web documents likely to change.
10. Any source added during copyedit requires an evidence-register entry and a corresponding reference.
