# Chapter 7 v0.1 — Developmental, Narrative and Book-Prose Review

**Chapter:** Capability Before Compliance  
**Date:** 2026-11-18  
**Decision:** **PASS — proceed to Forensic Evidence Survival and targeted Author Revision**

## Overall judgement

Chapter 7 v0.1 succeeds as a book chapter rather than a training chapter.

Its central achievement is to make capability visible as an operating condition of governance. The composite maintenance exercise creates a distinct organisational tension: every formal control appears present, yet the people responsible cannot convert training, signals and authority into timely judgement.

The chapter sustains one dominant proposition:

> **Capability is not merely preparation for AI governance. It is one of the mechanisms through which governance operates.**

The supporting distinction is also earned:

> **Compliance can show that training occurred. Capability asks whether judgement can still occur when it matters.**

The argument does not dismiss compliance or training. It distinguishes evidence of activity from evidence of performance in context.

## Argument

**Outcome: PASS**

The chapter answers the question left by Chapter 6: whether people carrying accountability can recognise when the control environment is no longer enough.

The argument develops coherently:

1. a green dashboard and high training-completion rate;
2. a realistic exercise in which no participant uses the available intervention route;
3. the distinction between training participation and training transfer;
4. AI literacy as necessary but incomplete;
5. domain judgement and complementary expertise;
6. calibrated reliance rather than maximum trust;
7. the limits of assumed human-AI synergy;
8. augmentation and expertise risk as alternative design outcomes;
9. manager capability;
10. voice and psychological permission;
11. collective capability and contextual evidence;
12. the handoff to Governance as Permission.

No material argument drift was identified.

## Architecture

**Outcome: PASS**

The chapter has a clear beginning, development and handoff.

The maintenance scenario remains active rather than disappearing after the opening. It returns at the major conceptual turns:

- training completion;
- interpretation of uncertainty;
- domain knowledge;
- reliance;
- management capability;
- silence and escalation;
- contextual evidence of capability.

This prevents the middle of the chapter becoming a catalogue of competencies.

## Narrative

**Outcome: PASS**

The opening is organisationally credible and different from Chapters 3–6. The tension arises from ordinary work pressure, system reliability, ambiguity and social cost rather than a dramatic AI failure.

The chapter's discovery sequence is effective. The reader first sees a compliant organisation, then realises that the operating assumption behind its controls has not been demonstrated.

The ending creates genuine forward pressure:

> **Does the governance system give capable people legitimate room to act?**

Chapter 8 follows as a consequence rather than a scheduled topic.

## Book prose

**Outcome: PASS WITH LIGHT REFINEMENT**

The prose is sustained, practical and evidence-aware. It avoids consultant-listicle form and does not read like an L&D handbook.

A small number of short paragraphs are used effectively for emphasis. The cadence is generally controlled. No structural rewrite is required.

Light revision should:

- identify the opening organisation explicitly as composite;
- smooth the transition from training transfer into AI literacy;
- preserve the distinction between a person sensing a problem and possessing enough evidence or authority to act;
- retain the final paragraphs without adding a summary checklist.

## Voice

**Outcome: PASS**

The chapter combines engineering discipline, adult-learning insight, organisational judgement and practical experience.

It does not romanticise professional intuition, dismiss formal training or frame employees as the sole control against flawed systems. Institutional conditions remain visible throughout.

## Theory and authenticity

**Outcome: PASS**

The chapter introduces no unnecessary new framework.

It correctly treats:

- AI literacy;
- training transfer;
- calibrated reliance;
- automation bias;
- expertise development;
- psychological safety;
- employee voice;

as established bodies of work.

The Gardner contribution is the integration of those fields into the book's meaningful-accountability argument.

## Engrossment

**Outcome: PASS**

The chapter remains engaging because the evidence changes the reader's interpretation of the tabletop exercise. The quantitative evidence is selective and purposeful rather than decorative.

The augmentation evidence prevents the chapter from becoming predictable or anti-AI.

It owns the human and organisational capability required for those prior arrangements to work.
