**Book:** AI Governance in Practice

**Format:** SVG master  
**Primary placement:** Chapter 9 — Evidence Before Assurance; reinforced in Chapter 10.

## Reader purpose
To show governance as a traceability and learning system connecting organisational purpose to delegated authority, operational boundaries, execution, surviving evidence, evaluation and organisational learning.

## Construct
**Purpose → Authority → Boundary → Execution → Evidence → Evaluation → Learning**

Learning creates a feedback loop into earlier governance decisions. **Assurance is outside the chain** and is a conclusion drawn from evaluation, not another stage.

## Interpretation control
The Evolved Governance Chain is a non-linear traceability spine, not a universal lifecycle. Successful execution is not itself assurance. Evaluation must consider surviving evidence, and learning must be capable of changing purpose, authority, boundaries or other governance conditions.

## Caption
**Evolved Governance Chain.** Governance connects purpose, authority and boundaries to execution, evidence, evaluation and learning. Learning feeds back into governance adaptation. Assurance is a conclusion drawn from evaluation rather than another link in the chain.

## Alt text
A seven-stage horizontal governance chain labelled Purpose, Authority, Boundary, Execution, Evidence, Evaluation and Learning. A feedback loop from Learning returns to earlier governance stages. A separate Assurance box sits below Evaluation, showing that assurance is derived from evaluation and is not an additional chain stage.
