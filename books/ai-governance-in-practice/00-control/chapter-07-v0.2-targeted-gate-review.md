## Evidence Survival

**Outcome: PASS**

The manuscript visibly retains the complete evidence backbone:

- AI literacy includes understanding, use, evaluation and ethics;
- training completion and transfer into work are different conditions;
- appropriate reliance differs from maximum trust;
- automation bias and over-reliance are context-dependent;
- explanations and confidence displays do not consistently improve judgement;
- human-AI combination is not automatically synergistic;
- AI can improve performance and benefit less experienced workers in some settings;
- generative AI can change cognitive effort and scrutiny;
- manager capability is role-specific;
- psychological safety and leader response influence voice;
- capability may be collective and distributed;
- evidence of capability should be proportionate and contextual.

The chapter retains the important counterevidence that training can work and AI can augment capability.
