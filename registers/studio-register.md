## Register rules

1. A Studio must not be described as governed or approved unless its control record is approved and linked here.
2. Material changes to purpose, classification, ownership, connectors or authority require a register update.
3. Retired and superseded Studios remain listed with their final status and archive location.
4. Student evidence, client confidential material and other restricted operational records must not be added to this register.
