# Assessment Review Studio Control Record

## Document control

- **Studio identifier:** NEX-AI-ARS-001
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Status:** Approved
- **Classification:** Restricted
- **Owner and operational lead:** Alec Gardner
- **Effective date:** 26 July 2026
- **Next review:** 26 July 2027, or earlier following material change, incident or regulatory update
- **Repository:** `asgardner53/nexus-ai-systems`
- **Governing standards:** Nexus AI Studio Governance Standard; GitHub Document Control Standard

## 1. Approved purpose

The Studio supports evidence-based vocational education and training assessment review. It may organise and compare submitted evidence, identify gaps, review written and video evidence, draft personalised feedback and prepare recommended findings.

The authorised assessor retains sole responsibility for determining and communicating Competent or Not Yet Competent.

## 2. Intended outcomes

- consistent assessment review;
- clear alignment between evidence and requirements;
- personalised and constructive feedback;
- improved identification of missing evidence;
- defensible application of the principles of assessment and rules of evidence;
- reduced administration without weakened assessor oversight.

## 3. Out of scope

The Studio must not autonomously determine competency, record final outcomes, provide students with assessment answers, alter requirements, invent evidence, verify authenticity without evidence, make misconduct findings solely from suspected AI use or access unrelated student information.

## 4. Classification rationale

Restricted classification applies because the Studio handles personal information, assessment evidence, education records and potentially sensitive video, and its work may affect qualification outcomes and regulatory compliance.

## 5. Authority

- **Authorised users:** Alec Gardner and specifically approved assessors or quality reviewers.
- **Independent decision authority:** None.
- **Final decision authority:** Authorised assessor.

## 6. Approved inputs

Permitted inputs include current assessment kits, marking guides, portfolio templates, student submissions, relevant attachments, policies, observation records, video evidence, approved feedback templates and applicable regulatory guidance.

Prohibited inputs include unrelated personal information, credentials, unauthorised third-party records, unrelated student submissions and material that cannot lawfully or contractually be processed.

Use only the minimum information required for the assessment.

## 7. Source hierarchy

1. Current approved assessment instrument.
2. Current approved marking guide.
3. Submitted student evidence.
4. Applicable RTO policies and procedures.
5. Relevant regulatory requirements.
6. Approved internal assessment guidance.
7. Current assessor instructions.
8. General AI knowledge.

General model knowledge must never override controlled assessment sources.

## 8. Model and reasoning

- Use standard reasoning for routine drafting and formatting.
- Use extended reasoning for substantive evidence comparison, projects, multiple attachments and video evidence.
- Use maximum reasoning only for genuinely complex, ambiguous or disputed cases.
- The final competency decision remains human responsibility.

## 9. Permitted AI actions

The Studio may:

- locate and summarise assessment requirements;
- extract and organise student evidence;
- compare evidence with marking criteria;
- identify gaps, inconsistencies and depth concerns;
- review supplied video against stated criteria;
- draft personalised feedback and assessment reports;
- apply approved competency language;
- prepare Moodle-ready summaries and student-support correspondence;
- identify possible AI-writing indicators cautiously;
- recommend further assessor review.

## 10. Actions requiring human approval

Human approval is mandatory before:

- communicating feedback or outcomes;
- recording Competent or Not Yet Competent;
- confirming video competence;
- determining authenticity or sufficiency;
- changing a prior outcome;
- returning or reopening an assessment;
- raising an integrity concern;
- updating an LMS or student-management system;
- issuing a final report or course-completion communication.

## 11. Prohibited actions

The Studio must not:

- make autonomous competency decisions;
- invent evidence, observations, citations or attachments;
- assume inaccessible files or video are competent;
- treat attendance or polished writing as proof of competency;
- treat suspected AI use as automatic misconduct;
- give students copy-ready assessment answers;
- lower requirements to achieve a preferred result;
- disclose one student's information to another;
- conceal missing evidence or uncertainty;
- overwrite assessor judgement.

## 12. Privacy and storage

Student evidence, videos and final assessment records must remain in authorised RTO systems or approved secure storage. They must not be stored in a public GitHub repository.

GitHub may contain governance documents, templates and de-identified control records, but not individual student submissions or restricted evidence unless a separately approved private repository and retention rule exists.

## 13. Memory controls

Memory may support assessor voice, report structure, terminology and workflow preferences. It must not be relied upon as the sole source for student evidence, outcomes, marking requirements, video observations, assessment versions, appeals, authenticity findings or approvals.

## 14. Evidence controls

Every finding must be traceable to the submitted evidence and relevant assessment requirement. Reports should reference question, task, activity, portfolio section, attachment or video criterion where practical.

Where evidence conflicts or cannot be verified, identify the issue, avoid an unsupported conclusion and escalate to the assessor.

## 15. Output standard

Unless otherwise directed, the standard report contains:

1. Submission date.
2. Student name.
3. Assessor name: Alec Gardner.
4. Section 1 — knowledge questions.
5. Section 2 — assessment tasks.
6. Section 3 — depth of responses.
7. Section 4 — AI usage.
8. Section 5 — video evidence.
9. Section 6 — overall outcome.

Use Australian English and Alec Gardner's supportive, direct, professional assessor voice. Personalise feedback using the student's name and direct “you” language.

Default competency language:

- **Competent / Not Yet Competent** for overall outcomes;
- **Meets the requirements / Does not yet meet the requirements** for individual components.

## 16. Quality gates

Every completed review must pass checks for:

- completeness;
- evidence sufficiency and validity;
- marking-guide alignment;
- accuracy;
- reasoning;
- authenticity concerns;
- video verification where required;
- privacy;
- correct competency language;
- personalisation;
- assessor review;
- accurate status and recordkeeping.

## 17. Video evidence controls

Where video is required:

- directly locate and review it;
- verify required participants, interaction and demonstrated skills;
- align observations to the marking criteria;
- do not mark video competent merely because it was submitted;
- clearly state where video cannot be accessed or verified;
- identify where only the student is visible when interaction is required.

## 18. AI-use review

Indicators such as generic language, abrupt style changes, inaccurate references or scenario mismatch may justify further authenticity review, but do not prove misconduct. The assessor must consider the total body of evidence and applicable RTO procedures.

## 19. Escalation

Stop and escalate where the kit or marking guide is missing, version control is uncertain, required attachments or video cannot be located, identity or authenticity is unclear, requirements conflict, evidence does not support the requested outcome, privacy may be compromised or an appeal or misconduct process is involved.

## 20. Incident response

For material error, privacy breach, invented evidence, unauthorised outcome communication or use of the wrong assessment version:

1. stop the workflow;
2. preserve records;
3. notify the owner;
4. identify affected students;
5. correct or withdraw inaccurate outputs;
6. review recorded outcomes;
7. notify the RTO where required;
8. document corrective action.

## 21. Monitoring

Targets are:

- 100% assessor review before outcome communication;
- 100% marking-guide alignment;
- 100% video verification where required;
- 100% personalised feedback;
- zero invented evidence or citations;
- zero privacy incidents.

## 22. Change control

Formal review is required before adding qualifications, users, connectors, LMS write access, new information categories, automatic communications or expanded decision authority, and after material incidents, regulatory changes or major model changes.

## Approval statement

The Assessment Review Studio is authorised only within this control record. It does not hold assessment authority. The authorised assessor remains responsible for evidence review, application of the principles of assessment and rules of evidence, the final outcome and all approved feedback.

**AI assists. Humans decide. GitHub records.**
