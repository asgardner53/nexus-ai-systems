## Reader purpose
To show that meaningful accountability depends on five interdependent organisational conditions rather than a nominal human being assigned responsibility.

## Construct
- Capability
- Visibility
- Authority
- Intervention capacity
- Accountability alignment
- Centre: Meaningful Accountability

## Interpretation control
The five conditions are **interdependent, not additive**. This is not a score, maturity scale or validated quantitative instrument. A serious weakness in one condition can make accountability fragile even where other conditions appear strong.

## Caption
**Meaningful Accountability Test.** Accountability becomes meaningful when capability, visibility, authority, intervention capacity and accountability alignment remain connected in practice. The conditions are interdependent rather than additive.

## Alt text
A systems diagram with Meaningful Accountability in the centre, surrounded by five necessary conditions: Capability, Visibility, Authority, Intervention Capacity and Accountability Alignment. A note states that the conditions are interdependent, not additive, and that serious weakness in one can make accountability fragile.
