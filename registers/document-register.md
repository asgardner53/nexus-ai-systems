## Document status definitions

- **Draft:** under development and not approved for reliance.
- **Under Review:** submitted for approval or controlled review.
- **Approved:** authorised and currently effective.
- **Approved live register:** authorised and expected to be updated as controlled entries change.
- **Superseded:** replaced by a later approved version.
- **Suspended:** temporarily withdrawn from use.
- **Retired:** no longer authorised for operational use.

## Register rules

1. Only the version identified here as Approved or Approved live register is authoritative.
2. Drafts and working copies must not be represented as approved.
3. When a document is replaced, the new version must be added and the earlier version marked Superseded.
4. Material changes require an entry in the Change and Decision Register.
5. Deleted or retired documents must remain recoverable through Git history or controlled archive arrangements.
6. AIRBOK manuscript and doctrine documents are controlled in `asgardner53/airbok-foundational-edition` and should be recorded in that repository's manuscript and doctrine control records.
7. Student assessment evidence and confidential client records are not listed in this governance register.
