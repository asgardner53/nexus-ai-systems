# The Learning Frontier — Research and Publication Workspace

**Publisher:** Nexus BMG Research & Insight  
**Publication:** The Learning Frontier — AI, Human Capability & the Future of Education  
**Status:** Proposed repository structure, for human review; not an approved governance standard.

This directory is the proposed version-controlled home for Frontier Research Intelligence Agent (FRIA) outputs and The Learning Frontier Studio publication assets.

FRIA research delivery is scheduled Monday and Friday. Scheduled brief delivery does **not** itself persist outputs in GitHub; saving verified records requires a separate authorised workflow.

## Directories

- `00-governance/`: ownership, decision gates and control records.
- `01-research-intelligence/`: dated Monday/Friday research briefs.
- `02-evidence-register/`: claim-level verified evidence and source status.
- `03-editorial-pipeline/`: ranked editorial opportunities and decision log.
- `04-curriculum-innovation/`: proposed teaching, learning and assessment demonstrations.
- `05-newsletter-editions/`: edition status, manuscripts and publishing assets.
- `06-publication-quality-assurance/`: Platinum review, checks and approval records.

## Controls

Use the existing Nexus Evidence Engine for Search → Verify → Challenge → Register → Reuse → Monitor. Publication research is M2 or higher. Do not present an unverified study as established evidence. Preserve original URLs, dates, scope, contrary findings and confidence. Follow the existing Nexus document control and Platinum editorial standards; do not duplicate or supersede them. AI assists; humans decide. No publication or merge without human approval.
