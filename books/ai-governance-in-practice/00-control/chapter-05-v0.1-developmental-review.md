**Status reviewed:** Developmental Review  
**Decision:** **PASS SUBJECT TO TARGETED AUTHOR REVISION**

## Overall judgement

Chapter 5 performs its required job in the manuscript. It moves the book from the conditions required for meaningful human accountability to the organisational authority permitted to AI. The authority-drift opening is distinct from Chapters 3 and 4, the Delegated Authority Map is earned before it is introduced, and the final movement makes Chapter 6 necessary.

The chapter does not require a structural rewrite. It does require a targeted v0.2 revision before developmental approval because four limited defects remain:

1. the opening organisational case is illustrative but is not yet explicitly identified as composite;
2. **authority drift** is introduced as a named diagnostic without an explicit provenance boundary;
3. two broad formulations should be narrowed to the degree of certainty supported by the evidence;
4. several isolated short paragraphs should be absorbed into fuller paragraph movements.

## Argument and architecture

The chapter sustains one argument:

> Governance must define the operational authority an organisation permits an AI system to exercise, rather than infer authority from product labels or technical capability.

The sequence is coherent:

1. an approved assistant accumulates access and decision capacity;
2. labels fail to describe authority;
3. capability is separated from permission;
4. decisions are decomposed into functions;
5. the limits of a single autonomy ladder are established;
6. contextual delegation and authority drift are developed;
7. the Delegated Authority Map is introduced and applied;
8. control, recourse and accountability are distinguished;
9. the map's limits are preserved;
10. Chapter 6 is made necessary.

No movement is redundant. The chapter remains inside its boundary and does not become a technical implementation manual.

## Strongest elements

### The authority-drift opening

The cumulative implementation changes are plausible and recognisable. The system's label remains stable while its authority expands through integration, access and workflow decisions. This is a stronger opening than a dramatic agent failure because it locates the governance problem in ordinary organisational practice.

### Capability versus permission

The governing proposition is clear, memorable and practically useful. The engineering analogy is light and proportionate.

### Function-specific delegation

The use of Parasuraman, Sheridan and Wickens alongside MIT CISR provides a defensible bridge from established automation research to current organisational decision rights.

### The map is earned

The Delegated Authority Map is not revealed prematurely. It appears only after labels, autonomy ladders and static approval have been shown to be insufficient.

### Chapter 6 handoff

The distinction between a boundary described in an approval document and a boundary enforced in operating infrastructure provides genuine forward pressure.

## Priority revisions

### 1. Identify the opening as illustrative

The customer-service sequence is a composite scenario. The manuscript currently reads as though it may be a documented case.

Add a light disclosure without weakening the opening, for example:

> The organisation is composite, but the sequence is ordinary.

This closes the case-integrity issue while preserving narrative immediacy.

### 2. Control the provenance of *authority drift*

The manuscript currently states:

> This is authority drift...

Revise to make the interpretive source explicit:

> I use the term **authority drift** for the gradual movement between the authority originally approved and the authority a system comes to exercise...

The underlying change-control problem is evidence-supported. The label is Gardner descriptive synthesis and must not appear as externally validated terminology.

### 3. Narrow two broad formulations

Replace:

> The familiar intuition that more autonomy means more risk is also unreliable.

with a bounded formulation such as:

> The familiar intuition that more autonomy always means more risk can also mislead.

Replace:

> No formula can convert those factors into one universally correct autonomy level.

with:

> The current evidence does not provide a single, universally valid formula for converting those factors into one correct autonomy level.

These revisions preserve the argument while avoiding unsupported universality.

### 4. Smooth local paragraph fragmentation

Absorb selected short paragraphs into fuller movements, particularly:

- “A single autonomy label compresses these differences until they disappear.”
- “The governance consequence is not that organisations should resist automation. It is that they should delegate more precisely.”
- “The customer-service assistant had not escaped those principles...”
- “Permission without a boundary becomes discretion by accident.”
- “Recourse is therefore part of the original delegation design.”

The bold governing proposition and final question may remain as scarce, earned emphasis.

### 5. Preserve evidence-quality qualification

When citing MIT CISR's 2026 framework, briefly retain the research boundary: thirty interviews with twenty-seven executives across nine organisations. This avoids presenting a current executive framework as universal theory.

## Theory and originality

The Delegated Authority Map passes its provenance test. The manuscript explicitly acknowledges neighbouring automation, decision-rights and meaningful-human-control scholarship and states that the contribution is integration rather than discovery.

The following boundaries survive:

- the map is not a validated scientific instrument;
- it is not a legal test or transfer of legal personality;
- it is not a universal autonomy taxonomy;
- it is not a compliance certificate;
- passing the map does not establish that deployment is safe, fair, lawful or appropriate.

The only theory repair required is explicit classification of **authority drift** as Gardner descriptive language.

## Book-prose judgement

The chapter reads predominantly as book prose rather than a professional article. It sustains the opening example across the chapter, avoids excessive headings and does not reduce every movement to a visible lesson.

The local fragmentation is limited rather than systemic. A targeted prose revision is sufficient; no full rewrite is required.
