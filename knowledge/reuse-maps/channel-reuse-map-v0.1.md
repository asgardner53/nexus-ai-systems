## Purpose

Map reusable AI knowledge into possible Nexus destinations without implying that the knowledge or proposed use has been approved.

| Destination | Typical reuse | Minimum gate before execution |
|---|---|---|
| AIRBOK | research input, contradiction search, doctrine candidate investigation | AIRBOK research and doctrine controls |
| AI Edge | article, commentary, framework, case analysis | publication evidence and editorial review |
| The Evolving Leader | leadership implications, cases, executive application | publication evidence and editorial review |
| People Agenda | Australian HR/work implications | current Australian legal/regulatory evidence where material |
| LinkedIn | post, long-form article, visual concept | publication evidence and human approval |
| Masterclasses | activity, case, framework, diagnostic | learning-design review and evidence fit |
| Udemy / LinkedIn Learning | lesson, example, exercise | course-learning and platform controls |
| VET | teaching example, learner explanation, activity support | unit alignment; never add assessment requirements not in the unit |
| PD Studio | reflection prompt, currency evidence lead | professional-currency relevance and source traceability |
| Client advisory | diagnostic, workshop question, recommendation input | M2/M3 evidence check and client-context review |
| Small-business advisory | practical diagnostic, workflow opportunity, implementation aid | evidence and client-context review |
| Governance | control candidate, checklist, risk pattern | governance review and formal approval |
| Ebooks / books | argument, case, chapter concept | publication evidence, provenance and editorial controls |
| Product / Studio development | feature idea, workflow, control, architecture pattern | product governance, testing and human approval |

## Reuse rule

A destination may be recorded while the originating knowledge remains **Candidate**. External or consequential use must not occur until the required evidence and human gates for that destination are satisfied.
