# Nexus Book Intelligence Engine

## Purpose

The Nexus Book Intelligence Engine is the intellectual-control and assurance layer beneath the Nexus BMG Ebook Studio.

It does not replace the Studio and it does not duplicate the Nexus Evidence Engine.

- **Nexus BMG Ebook Studio** creates and shapes the book.
- **Nexus Book Intelligence Engine** controls the book.
- **Nexus Evidence Engine** researches, verifies and challenges external evidence.
- **GitHub** is the persistent controlled record.

## Core responsibilities

The Engine maintains:

1. Research & Evidence Spine
2. Claims & Evidence Register
3. Concept Provenance Register
4. Red-Team Intellectual Challenge
5. Architecture and chapter-to-chapter novelty control
6. Terminology and continuity controls
7. Platinum Gate state
8. Material author decisions
9. Publication-readiness control records

## Governing evidence rule

For consequential claims distinguish:

1. Evidence establishes
2. Reasonable interpretation
3. Gardner synthesis

Do not allow a source to appear to validate an original Gardner construct unless it actually does.

## Status logic

Use PASS, CONDITIONAL PASS, FAIL or N/A.

A FAIL stops dependent progression. A CONDITIONAL PASS must identify the unresolved item and closure point.
