## 7. Structured PD Journal draft

### Activity
**AI for Trainers and Assessors — professional development course**

### Date
10 October 2026

### Professional development objective
To strengthen understanding of practical and responsible AI use in training and assessment, with particular attention to source verification, professional judgement and appropriate human oversight.

### Key learning
The activity clarified the distinction between generative AI and predictive analytics and reinforced the need to verify AI-generated information against reliable source material before using it in professional work.

### Reflection
The participant recognised that AI can improve efficiency and support drafting, research and analysis, but that a polished AI response should not be treated as automatically correct. The reflection shows increased awareness of the need to retain professional judgement and to check important claims against original or authoritative sources.

### Application to practice
The participant intends to apply a more deliberate verification step when using AI-generated material in training and assessment work. This includes checking source material, distinguishing AI assistance from assessor judgement and avoiding over-reliance on AI-generated explanations.

### Relevance to professional currency
This activity contributes to current professional awareness of AI-enabled work practices relevant to training and assessment. It supports ongoing currency by helping the participant understand emerging tools, risks and governance expectations.

### Evidence retained
- course completion evidence where applicable;
- original spoken reflection;
- verified transcript;
- structured PD Journal entry.

### Follow-up action
Apply the verification approach in future training or assessment work and record a later reflection on how the approach affected practice.
