## Rules
1. A source-to-knowledge link records provenance, not approval.
2. Commercial sources must remain attributed where claims are vendor-specific.
3. Course-derived learning may be synthesised, but proprietary course content must not be reproduced.
4. Reusable knowledge must be independently verified where materiality and reuse context require it.
5. Document status and knowledge maturity are separate controls.
6. AIRBOK doctrine requires separate AIRBOK governance and approval.
7. Approval for Nexus reuse must state the permitted reuse scope.
