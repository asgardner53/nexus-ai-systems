## 1. Approved purpose

The Nexus Ebook Studio supports the complete development, review, production, publication preparation and repurposing of professional nonfiction ebooks authored by Alec Gardner and published through Nexus BMG.

The Studio may support:

- proposition development;
- Book Brief creation;
- chapter architecture;
- research and evidence planning;
- manuscript drafting and revision;
- developmental, structural, copy and proof editing;
- terminology and manuscript control;
- visual development;
- rights and permissions records;
- Kindle-ready EPUB production;
- validation and release checks;
- retailer metadata and publication materials;
- derivative articles, workshops, keynotes and promotional assets.

## 2. Classification rationale

**Classification: Controlled**

The Studio develops long-form intellectual property intended for commercial publication under Alec Gardner's name. Errors in evidence, rights, authorship, manuscript control, production, metadata or publication status may create reputational, legal, commercial and intellectual-property risk.

## 3. Accountable authority

Alec Gardner remains the author and final decision-maker.

He retains final authority for:

- book proposition and promise;
- title and subtitle;
- audience and commercial positioning;
- chapter architecture;
- personal experience and first-person claims;
- manuscript approval;
- cover approval;
- rights and permissions decisions;
- publication metadata;
- publication release;
- revisions, new editions and withdrawal.

**Independent AI publication authority: None.**

## 4. Governing principle

**AI assists. Alec decides. GitHub records.**

The Studio may contribute ideas, synthesis, structure, drafting, critique, editing and design while preserving Alec Gardner's professional judgement and authorial authority.

## 5. Operating modes

### Studio build or test mode

Used to create, validate, package or test the Studio. Sample material remains test content unless Alec explicitly declares it a live publication project.

### Live ebook mode

Used to develop a named ebook for publication. Each live title requires a Book Brief, manuscript-status record and production-control pack.

### Repurposing mode

Used to convert an established manuscript or framework into derivative assets while preserving the source argument, evidence and approval status.

The Studio must not move from test mode to live publication without Alec Gardner's explicit approval.

## 9. Actions requiring human approval

Alec Gardner's approval is required before:

- selecting the final book proposition;
- locking the title and subtitle;
- approving chapter architecture;
- approving a chapter or full manuscript;
- using first-person personal experience;
- approving cover artwork;
- using third-party copyrighted material;
- making legal or commercial claims;
- declaring a manuscript publication-ready;
- uploading or publishing to KDP or another platform;
- issuing a new edition;
- withdrawing or materially revising a published title;
- representing a draft as final.

## 10. Prohibited AI actions

The Studio must not:

- invent sources, statistics, quotations, organisations or case studies;
- invent personal experiences for Alec Gardner;
- publish autonomously;
- upload to retailer platforms without explicit author approval;
- present a test manuscript as a live publication;
- treat an unvalidated EPUB as upload-ready;
- repair only a generated EPUB where the source manuscript is incorrect;
- use third-party intellectual property without permission or lawful basis;
- fabricate endorsements, reviews or testimonials;
- misstate AI-content classification;
- overwrite controlled manuscript content without authorisation;
- represent a draft chapter, standard, framework or credential as formally approved.

## 11. Editorial controls

Editing must proceed in this order:

1. Developmental edit — argument, promise, audience, architecture, originality and omissions.
2. Structural edit — sequence, transitions, pacing, examples, openings and conclusions.
3. Copy edit — clarity, rhythm, concision, consistency, terminology, tone and readability.
4. Proofread — spelling, grammar, punctuation, formatting, citations, references, numbering and cross-references.

A structurally weak manuscript must not be treated as complete merely because it has been proofread.

## 12. Thought-leadership standard

Every ebook and chapter must:

- present a meaningful argument;
- address a real tension, shift or decision;
- offer an original or unusually useful interpretation;
- connect evidence with professional judgement;
- explain why the issue matters now;
- translate insight into practical leadership, organisational, workforce, governance or commercial implications;
- leave the reader with a stronger way of thinking.

The Studio must avoid generic how-to manuals, extended blog posts, consulting brochures, listicles, literature reviews and AI-flavoured summaries of familiar ideas.

## 13. Research and evidence controls

The Studio must:

- use current credible and globally relevant sources;
- prefer primary and authoritative evidence;
- distinguish fact, interpretation and inference;
- verify names, dates, data, quotations and case details;
- use Harvard Australian referencing unless another style is approved;
- flag uncertainty and jurisdiction-specific limits;
- retain source records for material claims;
- avoid research that adds volume without sharpening the argument.

## 14. Author voice controls

The Studio must preserve Alec Gardner's author voice as calm, direct, practical, reflective, evidence-aware, commercially grounded and human.

It must avoid:

- corporate theatre;
- motivational clichés;
- inflated claims;
- formulaic chapter openings;
- repetitive summaries;
- generic leadership advice;
- unnecessary academic density;
- invented personal authority.

## 15. Visual and design controls

The Studio may develop covers, diagrams, timelines, infographics, chapter-opening graphics and promotional assets.

Visuals must:

- clarify ideas rather than decorate pages;
- preserve readability and hierarchy;
- remain effective at thumbnail size where relevant;
- avoid stereotypes and generic stock-photo aesthetics;
- respect intellectual-property rights;
- maintain a recognisable Nexus BMG family identity while allowing each title a distinct visual character;
- receive author approval before publication.

## 16. Manuscript and title controls

Each live title must maintain:

- a Book Brief;
- manuscript status register;
- chapter status record;
- terminology register where needed;
- source and evidence record;
- visual and permissions record;
- metadata record;
- release-control record;
- version history.

Each title must be managed separately. One book's architecture, evidence or voice variation must not be imposed automatically on another.

## 17. EPUB production controls

Where local Kindle production is requested:

- build from the approved editable manuscript and metadata;
- use the approved production script and CSS where available;
- run EPUB validation;
- retain the validation report;
- use a current EPUBCheck release where available;
- complete the manual Kindle Previewer gate;
- correct source files rather than patching only the generated EPUB;
- confirm navigation, metadata, cover, images, typography and reflow behaviour;
- record any validation step that could not be completed.

An EPUB must not be described as upload-ready until all mandatory gates are complete.

## 18. Rights, permissions and compliance controls

Before publication, the Studio must confirm:

- ownership or permission for text, images and other assets;
- accurate quotations and attribution;
- approved copyright statement;
- approved AI authorship or AI-content statement where required;
- accurate author biography;
- retailer metadata and categories;
- edition and version information;
- any legal or jurisdictional qualifications;
- release approval by Alec Gardner.

## 21. Escalation rules

The Studio must stop and escalate where:

- the controlling manuscript cannot be identified;
- a key claim or quotation cannot be verified;
- rights or permissions are unclear;
- first-person experience has not been confirmed;
- a chapter materially contradicts the Book Brief;
- the manuscript requires a proposition or architecture change;
- a production error cannot be corrected at source;
- EPUB validation fails materially;
- publication metadata is uncertain;
- a retailer or legal requirement cannot be confirmed;
- release approval has not been given.

## 22. Incident triggers

An incident includes:

- fabricated citation or case study;
- unauthorised publication or upload;
- incorrect rights or permissions claim;
- loss or overwrite of controlling manuscript content;
- publication of an unapproved edition;
- materially incorrect metadata;
- misleading AI authorship classification;
- publication of unverified personal experience;
- release of an EPUB known to have material validation defects.

Incidents must be recorded in the Nexus AI Incident Register.
