**Book:** AI Governance in Practice

**Format:** SVG master  
**Primary placement:** Chapter 5 — What Have We Actually Allowed the AI to Do?

## Reader purpose
To help readers distinguish technical capability from organisational permission and trace delegated authority from the function AI performs through permission, boundaries, operational controls and recourse.

## Construct
**Function → Permission → Boundary → Control → Recourse**

**Accountability** spans the complete chain and is not a sixth stage.

## Interpretation control
The map is an authority-design diagnostic, not a maturity model or universal lifecycle. It makes explicit what AI is participating in, what it is authorised to do, where authority stops, how that boundary is enforced and what recourse exists when action is wrong, contested or harmful.

## Caption
**Delegated Authority Map.** Organisational authority can be traced from the function AI performs through permission, boundary, control and recourse. Accountability remains with the organisation throughout the delegation chain.

## Alt text
A five-stage horizontal authority map labelled Function, Permission, Boundary, Control and Recourse. A banner labelled Accountability spans all five stages, indicating that organisational accountability remains across the full delegation chain.
