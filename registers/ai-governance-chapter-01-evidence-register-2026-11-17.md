## Citation-analysis record

Scite was used to verify four current shadow-AI publications. All four publication identities were verified, with no editorial notices or predatory-journal flags detected. Only Petersson et al. was selected to carry a chapter claim because it was directly relevant, accessible and empirically grounded. Three other recent papers were excluded from carrying core claims because they were too new, inaccessible, weakly cited or unnecessary to improve the evidence position.

The Scite selection log recorded eight included sources and five exclusions across official, academic, media and vendor material.
