## New descriptive construct — Authority drift

### Evidence establishes

NIST and Australian guidance require change management, continuing monitoring, reassessment after material change, access and permission control, and decommissioning. The Chapter 5 evidence record also identifies configuration, capability, purpose and access changes as reasons to revisit delegated authority.

### Reasonable interpretation

The authority exercised in practice can move away from the authority described in an earlier approval as systems, permissions, workflows and reliance change.

### Gardner synthesis

> **Authority drift:** the gradual movement between the authority originally approved and the authority a system comes to exercise through changes in capability, access, workflow, purpose or reliance.

### Provenance boundary

The underlying change-control problem is evidence-supported. The term is Gardner descriptive synthesis and is not presented as a validated construct, unique discovery or legal category.
